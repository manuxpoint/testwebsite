Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How quickly can I integrate Xpoint?

Most operators integrate Xpoint within a single sprint. Our developer-friendly SDKs and comprehensive documentation make the process straightforward.

What platforms do you support?

We support iOS, Android, Web browsers, and Desktop applications. Our cross-platform SDKs ensure consistent behavior across all your products.

How does pricing work?

We price based on monthly active users, not per-location check. This makes costs predictable and allows you to check player location as often as needed.

Which states do you support?

We support all 38+ states where sports betting is legal, plus Washington D.C. Our coverage automatically expands as new states come online.

Do you offer a sandbox environment?

Yes! We provide a full sandbox environment for testing your integration before going live. You can simulate various scenarios including edge cases.
